The family suite has two bedrooms joined by a small sitting area: a king room for parents and a twin room for children, each with its own bathroom. Both rooms have air conditioning, which helps on April and May afternoons.

It faces the pool lawn, so parents can sit on the verandah while children play within sight. The shallow end of the pool is 0.9 m deep. For groups of four adults the twin room works just as well, and we can set up a fifth mattress for a small extra charge.

## Good to know

- Ground floor, level path to the restaurant and pool.
- Cot and high chair available on request.
- Board games and a carrom board are in the sitting area.
