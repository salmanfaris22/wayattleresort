The pool villa sits on its own at the highest point of the estate, so the only view from the water is the tea valley below and the Chembra range on clear mornings. The plunge pool is about 4 m by 2.5 m and is not heated; in December and January the water is cold until mid-morning.

Inside there is a king bed, a writing table, a bathtub beside a window, and a rain shower. Breakfast can be served on the sit-out at no extra cost. Most couples book this villa for a honeymoon or an anniversary, so we keep the path to it quiet and do not route plantation walks past it.

## Good to know

- About 120 steps up from the parking area; staff carry your bags.
- Candle-light dinner on the deck can be arranged a day ahead.
- The nearest neighbour is the tree house, 60 m away through coffee.
