Wayanad is one of the best places in Kerala for a tree house night. They range from simple bamboo rooms on stilts to luxury tree houses with air conditioning and a bathtub.

## What to check before you book

Every tree house has stairs, and some have a steep ladder, so they do not suit older guests or small children. Ask how high the room is, whether the bathroom is inside the tree house or at the bottom, and whether there is hot water and power backup. In the monsoon (June to September) the forest is at its greenest, but paths get slippery and leeches are common.

## Good to know

- Many tree houses take only two guests; ask us for family options.
- Most couples book one tree house night and spend the other nights in a cottage or villa.
- Vythiri and the forest edge near Meppadi have the most tree houses.
