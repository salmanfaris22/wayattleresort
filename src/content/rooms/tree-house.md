The tree house is built around three silver oaks at the edge of the coffee block. A fixed wooden staircase with handrails takes you about 6 m up to a single room with a queen bed, a small sitting corner and a balcony at canopy height. It has a full attached bathroom with hot water.

Mornings are the reason to book it. Malabar whistling thrushes, barbets and bulbuls work through the branches outside, and on clear days you can watch the mist pull back from the valley without leaving bed. The room sways slightly in strong monsoon wind, which most guests enjoy.

## Good to know

- No air conditioning; at this height it stays cool all year.
- The staircase is steep, so we do not recommend it for small children or guests with knee trouble.
- Mosquito nets and repellent are provided.
