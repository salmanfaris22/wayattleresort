## The route, stage by stage

| Stage | Distance | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru → Mysuru | about 145 km | 2–2.5 hr | Bengaluru–Mysuru expressway; slow leaving the city |
| Mysuru → Gundlupet | about 60 km | 1–1.5 hr | Last fuel and food before the forest |
| Gundlupet → Muthanga (Kerala border) | about 30 km | 45 min | Bandipur forest, closed 9 pm to 6 am |
| Muthanga → Sulthan Bathery → Kalpetta | about 40 km | 1 hr | Busy towns, good lunch stops |
| Kalpetta → Vythiri | about 15 km | 30 min | NH 766 towards Kozhikode |

Distances and times are approximate and grow on holiday weekends.

## Driving through Bandipur and Muthanga

This stretch is the highlight and the main constraint. Elephants, deer and gaur often cross the road, so keep to the speed limit, do not stop, and never feed animals. The forest department enforces the night closure strictly; arriving at the barrier after 9 pm means a long wait or a long detour.

## Other ways to get there

- **By air:** fly from Bengaluru to Kozhikode (CCJ) or Kannur (CNN) and drive up, about 3 hours.
- **By train:** take a train to Mysuru and a taxi from there, or to Kozhikode and drive up the Thamarassery Ghat.
- **By bus:** state and private buses run to Sulthan Bathery and Kalpetta; take a taxi for the last stretch to Vythiri.

## Arriving at the resort

Vythiri is about 15 km past Kalpetta on the road towards Kozhikode. If you arrive before check-in, message us on WhatsApp; you can leave your bags and have lunch while the room is prepared.
