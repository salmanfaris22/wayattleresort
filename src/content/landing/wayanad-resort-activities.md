## Activities at the resort

- **Campfire.** The most asked-for evening activity. Most resorts light it on a lawn after dinner, weather permitting.
- **Barbecue.** Chicken, fish or paneer grilled on the lawn, usually ordered a day ahead.
- **Plantation walk.** A morning walk through tea, coffee and pepper with the estate staff.
- **Birdwatching.** Hornbills, barbets and the Malabar whistling thrush are common at forest-edge resorts.
- **Indoor games.** Carrom, table tennis and board games for rainy afternoons.

## Activities around Wayanad

- **Chembra Peak trek** to the heart-shaped lake, when the forest department has it open.
- **Banasura Sagar Dam** speedboats and a hill walk.
- **Kuruva Island** bamboo rafts on the Kabini river, in season.
- **Muthanga and Tholpetty** jeep safaris for elephants, deer and, with luck, a tiger.
- **Edakkal Caves** and their carvings, near Sulthan Bathery.
