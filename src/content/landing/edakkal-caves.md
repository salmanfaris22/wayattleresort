## What you will see

The "cave" is really a deep cleft between huge boulders, roofed over by a fallen rock. Its walls are covered with carvings: stylised human figures, animals, wheels and other symbols, layered over a very long period. Light changes through the day, and the carvings show most clearly when sun strikes the walls at an angle.

## Visiting tips

- Arrive in the morning; the climb is hot by midday and queues build on weekends.
- Wear shoes with grip and carry water; there is little on the way up.
- Photography rules can change, so ask at the ticket counter.
- Keep to the marked path and do not touch the carvings.
