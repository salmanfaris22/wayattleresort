## What the price includes

Every rate includes breakfast for the guests in the room, Wi-Fi, parking inside the estate, use of the outdoor pool, and the morning plantation walk. The Private Pool Villa also includes its own plunge pool.

**Not included:** lunch and dinner, barbecue nights, Ayurveda massages, jeep and tempo traveller hire, and entry or permit fees at places like Chembra Peak, Banasura Sagar Dam and the wildlife sanctuaries.

## How group prices work

Groups pay per room, not per person, so the cost per head falls as rooms fill up. The table of group sizes above is worked out from our real rooms: it picks the fewest rooms and the lowest total for each group size, including extra mattresses where a room allows one. For the largest groups, booking the whole estate also keeps the pool and campfire private.

## How to get a better rate

- Travel on weekdays, or in the monsoon when the hills are greenest.
- Stay longer; ask us about rates for three nights or more.
- Fill rooms well. A family of five in the family suite costs less per person than two smaller rooms.
- Book direct on WhatsApp, which avoids booking-site commission.

## Budgeting the rest of your Wayanad trip

Most visitors drive up from Kozhikode, Kannur, Mysuru or Bengaluru. Fuel, a meal on the way and entry tickets add up, so plan for them. Entry to places like Pookode Lake, Edakkal Caves and Banasura Sagar Dam is charged per person, with extra fees for boats and cameras, and forest-department treks and safaris have their own fees. Many of these places accept only cash or UPI, and mobile signal drops in the forest areas.
