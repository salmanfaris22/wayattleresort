## Route options

| Option | How | Total time |
|---|---|---|
| Drive | Kochi → Thrissur → Kozhikode → Thamarassery Ghat → Vythiri | 6–7 hr |
| Train + taxi | Ernakulam → Kozhikode by train, then taxi to Vythiri | 6–6.5 hr |
| Bus | KSRTC to Kozhikode or Kalpetta, then local bus or taxi | 7–8 hr |

Times are approximate and depend heavily on traffic around Kochi, Thrissur and Kozhikode.

## Driving tips

Start early to clear Kochi and Thrissur before traffic builds. The last stretch up the Thamarassery Ghat is best done in daylight; in the monsoon, mist and slow trucks can add an hour. Fuel up in Kozhikode or Thamarassery.

## By train

Many trains run between Ernakulam and Kozhikode through the day. From Kozhikode station, it is about 2 hours by taxi to Vythiri. Ask us when you book and we will tell you how to reach your resort from the station.

## Arriving in Vythiri

Vythiri is the first town after the top of the ghat, so resorts there are the quickest to reach from Kochi. From here, Pookode Lake is ten minutes away and Chembra Peak and Soochipara Falls are under an hour.

## Combining Wayanad with the rest of Kerala

Many travellers pair Kochi with Wayanad on a longer Kerala trip. A simple loop is Kochi (two nights) → Wayanad (two or three nights) → back via Kozhikode, or continue north to Kannur and its beaches. If you are coming from Munnar or Alappuzha, Kochi is the natural midpoint, so break the journey there rather than driving straight through.
