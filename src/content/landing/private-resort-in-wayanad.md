## Why book a private resort in Wayanad

A private stay suits families who want to relax without strangers at the pool, friends who want their own lawn for a campfire, and couples who want a villa where nobody walks past. You get the space of a resort with the privacy of a home.

## What to check before you book

- **Is it fully private?** Some "private" villas share a gate, a pool or a dining hall. Ask exactly what is yours.
- **Who looks after you?** Find out whether there is a caretaker on site at night and who cooks.
- **How is the road?** Private estates are often at the end of narrow plantation roads. Check your vehicle can reach.
- **What are the rules?** Music, visitors and campfires have limits at most villas.
