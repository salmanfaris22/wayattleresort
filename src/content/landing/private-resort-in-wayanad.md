## Two ways to stay privately

People who search for a private resort in Wayanad usually want one of two things. Families and groups of friends want **the whole place to themselves**: no strangers at the pool, a campfire just for them, and staff cooking for one group. Couples want **a private room with a private pool**, where the only people they see are each other.

Mistvale offers both. A whole-resort booking gives your group every room on the estate. The Private Pool Villa gives two people (plus a small child) a stand-alone villa with its own plunge pool, even when other guests are staying.

## How a whole-resort booking works

1. Send your dates and the number of adults and children on WhatsApp.
2. We check that every room is free, hold them for 24 hours and send the total.
3. A part advance confirms the booking; the advance and cancellation policy is on our contact page.
4. A week before arrival we agree a menu with you, so the kitchen can shop for your group.
5. On arrival, check-in is done together and the estate is yours until check-out.

## What you get to yourselves

The pool and lawn, the campfire after dark, the restaurant and verandah, and the estate paths through tea, coffee and pepper. Breakfast is included for everyone. Lunch, dinner and a barbecue night can be arranged as a set menu for the group.

## Good to know before you book

The estate is a working plantation on a hillside, so some rooms are up steps; tell us if anyone in your group cannot climb. Mobile signal is good near the restaurant and weaker in the tree house. Quiet hours start at 10 pm because neighbours and wildlife live close by.
