## What to do there

Most visitors walk the dam top first, then take a speedboat or pedal boat across the reservoir between the islands. The surrounding hills are part of the Banasura range, and on clear days you can see layers of ridges behind the water. Small stalls near the entrance sell tea, cut fruit and snacks.

## When to go

The reservoir is fullest after the monsoon, from September to December, when the islands are greenest. In March and April the water drops and more of the shore shows. Boat rides pause in strong wind and heavy rain.
