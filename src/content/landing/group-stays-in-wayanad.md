## What to check for a group trip

Groups have different problems from couples. Before you book anywhere in Wayanad, check these:

- **Rooms together.** Ten people in rooms spread across a hillside is a different trip from ten people around one lawn. Ask how the rooms are laid out.
- **Food for everyone.** Make sure the kitchen can serve the whole group at once, including vegetarian meals.
- **Vehicle access.** Many plantation roads are narrow and steep. A tempo traveller fits most places; a 40-seat bus does not.
- **House rules.** Campfire, music, alcohol and quiet hours vary between resorts. Find out before you pay the advance.

## Things groups like to do

Banasura Sagar Dam boating and the hill walk, the Chembra Peak trek when it is open, Edakkal Caves, Kuruva Island rafts in season, and a campfire night back at the resort. Jeep safaris in Muthanga and Tholpetty need an early start and fill fast on weekends.
