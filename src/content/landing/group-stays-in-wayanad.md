## How we split a group across rooms

Every room here has its own bathroom, which is what makes a group trip comfortable. When you send us your group size, we work out the plan with the fewest rooms and the lowest total, then adjust it for who is coming: couples get a room each where possible, families stay together, and anyone who cannot manage stairs gets a room on the level path.

The table above shows our current plans for common group sizes. It is worked out from our real rooms, so it never offers a bed that does not exist. Larger groups take the whole estate, which also means nobody else at the pool or the campfire.

## What groups do here

Evenings are the highlight for most groups: a campfire on the lawn, a barbecue if you order it a day ahead, and long conversations on the verandah. Days are a mix of the pool, the free morning walk through the estate, and trips out. Banasura Sagar Dam has speedboats that seat several people; Chembra Peak suits fit groups who can start at dawn; Edakkal Caves and Kuruva Island fill a full day further east.

## Food for a group

Breakfast is included. For the rest of the day, a set menu works best: rice, sambar, two vegetables, a fish or chicken curry, appam or parotta, and payasam on one day. Banana-leaf meals can be served for the whole group at lunch. Tell us about vegetarians, children and allergies when you book.

## House rules that keep group stays pleasant

- Quiet from 10 pm; no loudspeakers outdoors.
- Every guest shows a government photo ID at check-in.
- Damage to rooms or furniture is charged at cost.
- Group check-in is easiest when everyone arrives together; tell us if people come in separate cars.
