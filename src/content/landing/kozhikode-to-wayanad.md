## The route

| Stage | Distance | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Calicut airport (CCJ) → Kozhikode city | about 25–30 km | 45 min |
| Kozhikode → Thamarassery | about 35 km | 1 hr |
| Thamarassery Ghat → Lakkidi (top) | about 12 km | 30–45 min |
| Lakkidi → Vythiri | about 5 km | 10 min |

Distances and times are approximate; the ghat is slower on weekends.

## Driving the ghat

The Thamarassery Ghat (Thamarassery Churam) climbs from the plains to Wayanad's plateau in nine hairpin bends. In the monsoon, mist can settle on the upper bends, so keep headlights on and give way to buses. Heavy rain sometimes causes slides that close the road for a few hours; check before you set out on very wet days.

## Coming by train

Kozhikode railway station is on the main west coast line, with trains from Kochi, Mangaluru, Chennai and Bengaluru. From the station, take a taxi or a KSRTC bus towards Kalpetta; get off at Vythiri.

## Arriving in Wayanad

Vythiri is only a few minutes past the top of the ghat, which makes it the easiest part of Wayanad to reach from Kozhikode. Resorts in Vythiri and Lakkidi are the quickest to reach; we send you directions to your resort when you book.
