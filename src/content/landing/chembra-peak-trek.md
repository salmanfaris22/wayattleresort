|---|---|
| Height | About 2,100 m, the highest point in Wayanad |
| Start | Forest office area near Meppadi, south Wayanad |
| From Vythiri | About 45 minutes by road |
| Highlight | Heart-shaped lake on the way up |
| Run by | Kerala Forest Department with the local forest protection committee |
| Best months | Usually October to February, when open |

## How the permit works

Treks are guided and permits are issued at the forest office near the start of the trail, early in the morning. Numbers are limited each day, so arriving early matters, especially on weekends. Fees are charged per group plus per person, with a camera fee. Rules and fees change, so confirm them before you go.

## On the trail

The path leaves the tea estates and climbs into open grassland with wide views over the Wayanad plateau. The heart-shaped lake is the turnaround point for most treks. The upper slopes have little shade, so mornings are far more pleasant than afternoons. Stay with your guide and on the marked path; the grassland is fragile and fire risk is high in the dry months.

## Make a day of it

Soochipara Falls and Meenmutty Falls are both close to Meppadi, so a fit group can trek in the morning and visit a waterfall after lunch. Ask us for a resort near Meppadi if the trek is your main plan; many can pack breakfast for an early start.
