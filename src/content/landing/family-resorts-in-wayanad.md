## What a family needs from a Wayanad resort

Wayanad trips with children go best when the resort removes the small daily problems. Four things matter more than a long list of amenities:

- **Somewhere to sleep apart.** Two bedrooms, or at least a separate bed for the children, means everyone sleeps properly after a long drive up the ghat.
- **Food without a drive.** Outside the towns, few restaurants stay open after 8 pm. A kitchen on site that can make plain rice, dosa or an omelette at short notice is worth a lot.
- **A pool with a shallow end.** Children will spend half the trip in it. Check the depth before you book.
- **Short drives.** Wayanad is large. From Vythiri, Pookode Lake is ten minutes away, and Banasura Sagar Dam and Soochipara Falls are under an hour.

## Family rooms at Mistvale

The family suite is the room most families pick: a king room for parents, a twin room for children, a bathroom for each and a small sitting area with board games and a carrom board. It faces the pool lawn, so parents can sit on the verandah while children swim within sight.

Smaller families often take a plantation cottage, which has a king bed and space for a mattress. Families of six to eight usually book the family suite with a cottage next door; the plan table above shows how that works.

## A simple two-day plan with children

**Day one.** Arrive by lunch, swim in the afternoon, and join the campfire on the lawn after dark. **Day two.** Take the morning plantation walk (free for guests, about an hour), then drive to Pookode Lake for a pedal boat and the walking path around the water. Spend the afternoon at the pool and end the day early. If your children are older, add the speedboat at Banasura Sagar Dam or the bamboo raft at Kuruva Island, which is open outside the monsoon.

## Food for children

Breakfast is included: appam with stew or egg curry, puttu and kadala, dosa, toast and eggs. For lunch and dinner the kitchen cooks Kerala food with less spice on request, plus a few North Indian dishes. Tell us about allergies when you book, and we will plan around them.
