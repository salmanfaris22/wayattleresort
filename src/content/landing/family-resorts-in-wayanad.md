## What a family needs from a Wayanad resort

Wayanad trips with children go best when the resort removes the small daily problems. Four things matter more than a long list of amenities:

- **Somewhere to sleep apart.** Two bedrooms, or at least a separate bed for the children, means everyone sleeps properly after the long drive up the ghat.
- **Food without a drive.** Outside the towns, few restaurants stay open after 8 pm. A resort kitchen that can make plain rice, dosa or an omelette at short notice is worth a lot.
- **A pool with a shallow end.** Children spend half the trip in it. Check the depth before you book.
- **Short drives.** Wayanad is large. From Vythiri, Pookode Lake is ten minutes away, and Banasura Sagar Dam and Soochipara Falls are under an hour.

## A simple two-day plan with children

Day one: arrive by lunch, rest, then a pedal boat on Pookode Lake in the late afternoon. Day two: Banasura Sagar Dam in the morning for the speedboat and the walk along the dam, lunch, then the pool. If the children are older, add Soochipara Falls or the Kuruva Island bamboo rafts in season.
