## The route

| Stage | Distance | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mysuru → Nanjangud → Gundlupet | about 60 km | 1–1.5 hr |
| Gundlupet → Muthanga (forest, NH 766) | about 30 km | 45 min |
| Muthanga → Sulthan Bathery | about 15 km | 20 min |
| Sulthan Bathery → Kalpetta | about 25 km | 40 min |
| Kalpetta → Vythiri | about 15 km | 30 min |

Distances and times are approximate.

## When to leave

Leave Mysuru by early afternoon so you clear the forest well before the 9 pm closure and reach Vythiri before dark; the last hour is on hill roads. Early-morning departures are best for wildlife sightings.

## Stops worth making

Gundlupet is the last place for fuel and snacks before the forest. After the border, Sulthan Bathery has restaurants and is close to Edakkal Caves if you want to break the journey with a climb. Kalpetta, the district town, has ATMs and pharmacies.

## From Mysuru without a car

State buses run often from Mysuru to Sulthan Bathery and Kalpetta during the day. From Kalpetta, take a local bus or taxi to Vythiri. From Mysuru, resorts near Sulthan Bathery are the quickest to reach.
