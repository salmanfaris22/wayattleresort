## Distance from Mysuru to each Wayanad town

| Mysuru to | Distance | Drive time |
|---|---|---|
| Sulthan Bathery | 115 km | 2.5–3 hr |
| Mananthavady, via HD Kote | 115 km | 2.5–3 hr |
| Kalpetta | 140 km | 3–3.5 hr |
| Meppadi | 140 km | 3–3.5 hr |
| Vythiri | 150 km | 3.5–4 hr |

Approximate road distances from central Mysuru, measured with OpenStreetMap routing in November 2026. Every route except Mananthavady uses NH 766 through Gundlupet, the Bandipur and Muthanga forests and Sulthan Bathery. The Mananthavady road goes through HD Kote and Bavali and also crosses forest; check the Bavali check post closing time before you set out. Drive times allow for the slow forest stretch and town traffic; add time for stops.

| Stage | Distance | Time |
|---|---|---|

| Kalpetta → Vythiri | about 10 km | 20 min |

## When to leave

Leave Mysuru by early afternoon so you clear the forest well before the 9 pm closure and reach Vythiri before dark; the last hour is on hill roads. Early-morning departures are best for wildlife sightings.

## Stops worth making

Gundlupet is the last place for fuel and snacks before the forest. After the border, Sulthan Bathery has restaurants and is close to Edakkal Caves if you want to break the journey with a climb. Kalpetta, the district town, has ATMs and pharmacies.

## From Mysuru without a car

State buses run often from Mysuru to Sulthan Bathery and Kalpetta during the day. From Kalpetta, take a local bus or taxi to Vythiri. From Mysuru, resorts near Sulthan Bathery are the quickest to reach.
